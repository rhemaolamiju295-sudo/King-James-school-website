Academics

Discover your potential.

Three divisions, one journey — each stage designed for the age it serves.

Early Years

A joyful, play-based beginning where curiosity is protected and confidence takes root. Phonics, numeracy and discovery — led by specialist early-years teachers.

  • British & Nigerian blend curriculum
  • Small classes with dedicated assistants
  • Safe, purpose-built learning spaces

Primary

Strong foundations with growing independence. Pupils build mastery in literacy and numeracy while exploring science, the arts and sport.

  • Project-based learning
  • French and Yoruba from Year 3
  • Swimming and sport every week

Secondary

Ambitious academic preparation for WAEC, NECO and international examinations — with mentoring, leadership and service woven through the years.

  • Science, Arts and Commercial tracks
  • IGCSE preparation and exams
  • Career guidance from Year 9
